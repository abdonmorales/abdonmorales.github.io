Background

I'm pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Economics at the University of Texas at Austin, with an anticipated graduation in May 2027. My journey combines a strong academic foundation with hands-on experience in technology.

In the technical realm, I'm proficient in Git, C++, C, Python, and Linux, with intermediate abilities in shell scripting, AI, and ChatGPT. As a native Spanish speaker, I'm fluent in both Mexican and Spanish dialects, enabling me to communicate effectively in international contexts.

Currently, I'm expanding my knowledge in Java, Assembly language, C, and web development. My academic training includes courses such as Calculus I and II, Matrices and Matrix Calculations, Micro and Macroeconomics, and Engineering Physics, which have strengthened my analytical capabilities.

Outside the classroom, I actively develop software projects that can be seen on my GitHub profile. I've cultivated leadership skills as a team leader and CEO of my own startup, experiences that have allowed me to grow professionally.

In my free time, I enjoy soccer, video gaming, and programming as a hobby—activities that not only serve as creative outlets but also keep me grounded and connected to my community. My goal is to inspire positive change, highlighting education as a catalyst for progress. I firmly believe in seizing opportunities in our interconnected world and, through continuous learning, aspire to contribute significantly to the field of software engineering and beyond.
